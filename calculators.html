FA
Estimated GPA (4.0, unweighted)
—

An estimate, not your official GPA. There is no single official Ukraine-to-US conversion table — every US admissions office and credential evaluator (WES, ECE, Scholaro) uses its own thresholds, and they disagree at the edges. Use this number to plan; let the university or your evaluation service produce the official one. «Зараховано» (pass/credit) rows are correctly left out of the average, and US honors/AP weighting is not applied — Ukrainian transcripts don't carry those course types.
From the NMT

NMT → SAT/ACT Estimator

There is no official NMT-to-SAT table — so this tool does the honest version: it finds where your score sits among NMT takers, then reads the SAT and ACT score that sits at the same percentile. A rough range for planning, never a number for an application.

Leave a subject blank if you didn't take it — one score still gives a partial estimate. No score below 100 exists: if you didn't clear the threshold, there is nothing to convert yet.

Estimated ACT range
—

A rough, unofficial approximation. The NMT and the SAT/ACT are different tests taken by different populations — no official concordance exists. This estimate anchors your NMT percentile (official UCEQA NMT-2024 score statistics) to the same percentile on the College Board and ACT 2024 tables, which is a defensible starting point for prep planning and nothing more. Never self-report it as an SAT or ACT score.
