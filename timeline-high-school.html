Home · Leveled Timelines · High School

Four years,
sixteen seasons

Grades 9–12 — every season here has a deadline attached.

Grade 9 · Freshman

Start how you mean to finish

Fall

GPA begins now

Every grade counts toward your GPA from the first week.

Winter

Choose for four years

Pick activities you could still love as a senior; depth beats a long list.

Spring

Finish strong

Close the year well and ask which honors classes open up in 10th grade.

Summer

Purposeful summer

Volunteer, build, or study — admissions officers read summers.

Grade 10 · Sophomore

The rehearsal year

Fall

Practice PSAT

Take the PSAT for practice; the score doesn't count, the experience does.

Winter

Step up

Take a leadership role, however small, in one activity.

Spring

First college list

Draft a list of dream, match and safety schools — it will change, that's fine.

Summer

Start test prep

Begin gentle SAT prep, and TOEFL practice if English isn't your first language.

Grade 11 · Junior

The decisive year

Fall

Go all in

PSAT/NMSQT in October, plus the hardest course load you can carry well.

Winter

First real SAT

Sit the SAT, and start the scholarship hunt — note every deadline.

Spring

Ask early

Request recommendations from two teachers before summer break.

Summer

Draft the essay

Write your personal essay this summer — fall will be too busy.

Grade 12 · Senior

The harvest year

Fall

Applications open

Common App opens August 1; early deadlines land in November.

Winter

Money season

Regular decision closes in January — file FAFSA and finish scholarship essays.

Spring

Decisions arrive

Compare admission and aid offers carefully before the May 1 deposit.

Summer

The door opens

Visa interview, housing forms, packing — walk through it.
