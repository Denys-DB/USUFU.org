Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 July 2026.

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, used, and retained in connection with your visit to and use of the United States Universities For Ukraine website (the "Site"), which is operated by United States Universities For Ukraine, an independent educational initiative (the "Operator").

This Policy applies to the Site and to the free planning tools it offers, and it does not apply to any third-party website, service, or dataset that the Site may link to or reference.

By using the Site, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy.

The Site Does Not Require an Account

The Site does not require you to register, log in, or create any account, and it does not charge for or process any payments.

Planning Tools Operate Entirely in Your Own Browser

The Site offers a set of free educational planning tools, including a GPA calculator, a Ukrainian-grade-to-US-GPA converter, an NMT-to-SAT/ACT score estimator, a financial-aid finder, a true-cost and aid-letter decoder, an Education Record Helper, a jargon translator, and planning timelines and countdowns.

These tools run entirely inside your own web browser, and any information you type into them, along with any results they generate, is stored only on your own device using your browser's local storage or session storage.

The Operator does not receive, transmit, collect, or store any of the information you enter into these tools, and the Operator has no ability to view, recover, or restore that information if it is lost or deleted from your device.

You are responsible for safeguarding any device on which you use these tools, since information stored locally can be viewed by anyone with access to that device or browser profile.

The Contact Form

If you choose to contact the Operator through the Site's contact form, the Operator will receive only the information you voluntarily submit, which typically consists of your name, your email address, and the content of your message.

The contact form may be processed or delivered through a third-party form-handling or email-delivery service acting on the Operator's behalf, and such a service may incidentally process your submission as part of transmitting it to the Operator.

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your inquiry and for no other purpose.

The Operator does not use contact form submissions for marketing, profiling, or any automated decision-making.

No Sale or Sharing of Personal Data

The Operator does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party, and the Operator does not share your personal data with third parties except as necessary to deliver the contact form as described above or as required by law.

Donations

If you choose to make a donation, your payment is processed by a third-party payment provider away from the Site, and the Operator does not receive or store your card number or bank account details.

Cookies and Local Storage

The Site does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party analytics of any kind.

The Site enforces a strict Content Security Policy that limits the resources it loads to the Operator's own assets, a small number of web-font providers, and a small number of script content-delivery networks needed for the Site to function.

Any cookies or local storage used by the Site are first-party and strictly functional, meaning they exist only to support features such as language preference, tool calculations, and saved planning data on your own device.

You can clear this locally stored data at any time using your browser's settings, and doing so will not affect your ability to browse the Site.

Children's Privacy

The Site is an educational resource intended to be useful to students from middle school through university, and the Operator recognizes that some visitors will be minors.

The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 through the contact form or otherwise.

The Operator encourages minors to use the Site together with a parent, guardian, or teacher, and to seek that adult's involvement before submitting any information through the contact form.

If the Operator becomes aware that a child under 13 has submitted personal data through the contact form without appropriate consent, the Operator will delete that data promptly upon discovery.

International Visitors, Ukraine, and the European Union

The Site is accessed by visitors located in Ukraine, the European Union, the United States, and other countries, and data protection laws differ among these jurisdictions.

The Operator minimizes the personal data it collects by design, since the planning tools store data only on your own device and the Operator receives personal data only when you voluntarily submit the contact form.

Because most data associated with your use of the Site never leaves your own device, you can exercise control over that data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or cookies.

If you have submitted information through the contact form and wish to request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information, you may contact the Operator using the details below, and the Operator will respond within a reasonable time.

Third-Party Links

The Site links to universities, government agencies, scholarship providers, and other third-party resources for your convenience.

The Operator does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or accuracy of any third-party website, and this Policy does not apply to those third-party websites.

You should review the privacy policy of any third-party website before submitting personal data to it.

Security

The Operator uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect the Site and any information transmitted through the contact form.

No method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and the Operator cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

The Operator may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Site's practices or for legal or operational reasons.

The date at the top of this Policy indicates when it was last revised, and continued use of the Site after an update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about how your information is handled, you may contact the Operator at info@usufu.org.
