Home · Leveled Timelines · University & Beyond

From application
to graduation

The application year plus every stage of the degree — season by season.

Stage one

The application year

Fall

Everything converges

Applications, essays and interviews — everything you prepared for comes together.

Winter

Guard the offer

Submit financial aid documents and keep grades high; offers can be rescinded.

Spring

Say yes

Choose your university, pay the deposit, request the I-20.

Summer

Cross the ocean

F-1 visa interview, flights, and orientation registration.

Stage two

The first year

Fall

Be a face, not a name

Go to office hours in the first month — professors remember early faces.

Winter

Find your people

Study groups, clubs, and the international student office are your base.

Spring

Work early

Apply for on-campus jobs and summer internships before the rush.

Summer

Summer one

Internship, research or summer classes — momentum compounds.

Stage three

The middle years

Fall

Commit to a major

Declare it and map every credit you still need to graduate on time.

Winter

Two professors

Build real relationships with two professors who could write future references.

Spring

Real internships

Your resume is now real — apply for the serious ones.

Summer

The long interview

Treat internship season as a months-long job interview.

Stage four

The final year

Fall

Start immediately

Career fairs and job or grad-school applications begin in the first weeks.

Winter

Assemble the file

References lined up, resume polished, interviews practiced.

Spring

Graduation

The cap flies — and OPT paperwork if you plan to work in the U.S.

Summer

Every door stays open

First job, grad school, or home with a degree — the choice is finally yours.
